# Gochujang chicken ramen for four to six, shopped entirely at Woolworths

Serves 6. Prep 25 minutes, cook 35 minutes. Scaling for 4 is at the end.

Measurements follow the South African convention: **T is a tablespoon (15 ml), t is a teaspoon (5 ml)**.

## Ingredients

### Broth

- 1.5 L Fresh Free Range Liquid Chicken Stock
- 500 ml water
- 150 ml Asian Chicken Ramen Broth Base, the whole bottle
- 3 T Hikari Karu-Mu Koji Miso Paste
- 4 T gochujang paste
- 2 T sesame oil
- 6 cloves garlic, finely grated
- 30 g fresh ginger, finely grated
- 1 t sugar

### Chicken

- 6 free-range chicken thighs, skin on, deboned, about 1.1 kg
- 6 T light soy sauce
- 1 T sugar

### Noodles and toppings

- 3 x 200 g packs Ramen Noodles
- 500 g mushrooms, thick-sliced
- 2 T butter
- 2 T dark soy sauce
- 300 g baby spinach
- 6 eggs
- 100 g spring onions, finely sliced
- White sesame seeds, toasted

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken.** Mix 4 T light soy sauce with the sugar. Add the thighs and massage for 30 seconds until fully coated. Leave at room temperature while you do everything else, or overnight in the fridge. Reserve the other 2 T soy sauce.
2. **Soft-boil the eggs.** Six and a half minutes in boiling water, then straight into iced water. Peel once cold, halve just before serving.
3. **Bloom the aromatics.** In your largest pot, warm the sesame oil over medium heat. Add the garlic and ginger, fry 60 to 90 seconds until fragrant but not browned. Add the gochujang and the teaspoon of sugar and fry another two minutes, stirring, until it darkens slightly and smells sweet rather than sharp. Do not skip this. Frying the paste is what stops the broth tasting raw and sharp.
4. **Add the liquid.** Pour in the chicken stock, the water, and the whole bottle of ramen broth base. Bring to a bare simmer, not a rolling boil, and hold there for 20 minutes.
5. **Whisk in the miso last.** Ladle a cup of hot broth into a bowl, whisk the miso into it until completely smooth, then stir that back into the pot. Do not boil it after this. Taste: more gochujang for heat, more miso for depth, a splash of soy for salt.
6. **Cook the chicken.** Air-fryer at 180C, skin-side down, 15 minutes, turning halfway, in batches of two or three so they are not crowded. Or in a pan: dry heavy pan over medium-high, thighs skin-side down and pressed flat, 7 to 8 minutes until the skin is deep golden, then 4 to 5 on the other side. Rest five minutes, then slice thinly.
7. **The mushrooms.** Slice about 5 mm thick and dry-sear in a wide pan in batches, no oil and no lid, until they have given up their water and browned at the edges. Add the butter, cook a minute, then the dark soy sauce until the liquid has gone and they are glazed. Tip into a bowl.
8. **Greens, then the tare.** Same pan on low: wilt the spinach, stirring constantly, and pull it out the moment it collapses. Remove. Turn the heat to high, add the reserved 2 T light soy sauce, and reduce for one to two minutes to a thick caramel. This goes in the bottom of the bowls.
9. **Noodles, last.** Boil a large pot of water, cook the noodles 3 to 4 minutes, drain, serve immediately. Do not cook them in the broth and do not let them stand in liquid.
10. **Assemble.** Soy caramel into six warmed bowls, then noodles, then hot broth ladled over. Arrange chicken, mushrooms, spinach and half an egg on top. Finish with spring onions, sesame seeds, and a few drops of sesame oil.

Timing: steps 1 and 2 can happen hours ahead. The broth holds on a low flame and only improves, but do not boil it once the miso is in. Chicken, mushrooms and greens hold for 20 minutes. Cook the noodles once everyone is sitting down.

## Adjustments

- **Too spicy.** A teaspoon of honey or sugar into the broth. Gochujang's heat mellows against sweetness, not dairy.
- **Creamy.** A splash of coconut milk or 2 T cream at the end, off the heat.
- **Free depth.** Char an onion half and a few spring onion roots in a dry pan until blackened, simmer in the broth, fish out before serving.
- **Nutty body.** A tablespoon of tahini or smooth peanut butter whisked in with the miso.
