## Why `_redirects` is safe

The new catchall `/*  /section  200` sits at the end of `_redirects`, after `/invisibleink/*`, `/tarot/*`, `/birdle/*` (which still take priority since first-match-wins). Every other app (`bird-bingo`, `hardle`, `chessle`, etc.) has its own `index.html` in its own folder, and Cloudflare Pages serves real static files/folders before `_redirects` is even consulted - so those routes are unaffected without needing explicit rules, exactly as before the move.
