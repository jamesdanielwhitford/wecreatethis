The published post rendered wrong for its whole life and is now fixed: bold-led paragraphs were never wrapped in `<p>` (22 of them, merging into run-on text), tables and ordered lists were unsupported despite being used, its table of contents linked to heading ids that did not exist, the title rendered twice, and `//` inside URLs was greyed out as a comment. SW shell is now stale-while-revalidate with the shared `/sw-toast.js` pill; it was cache-first with no revalidation, which is why Safari showed new dark mode with the old renderer after a deploy.
- **Blog CLS bug FIXED and LIVE IN PROD** (session 011, `15d2c38`, SW `blog-v12`). The reveal frontier from `session-010-cls-diagnosis.md` is implemented: `revealLoaded()` in `app.js` reveals a post only once it and every post above it have rendered, with `.post-entry:not(.is-revealed) { visibility: hidden }` (not `display: none`, which would break the IntersectionObserver). **Live `/blog/test` now measures CLS 0.000 and Lighthouse 100/100/100/100 - Agentic Browsing went 80 → 100**, verified both with the SW defeated and with it active. Also verified locally on post deep link, heading anchor, sort toggle, a single-post section in dark mode, and a nested section, plus a forced-failure test confirming a failed fetch does not stall the frontier. Root cause was that the stack paints in manifest order but posts render in network-completion order, so the long first post landing late shoved already-painted siblings out of the viewport. **Height reservation cannot fix this and should never be retried** - estimates are viewport-width-dependent, and `content-visibility` measured 0.456, worse than the bug. Reasoning preserved in `session-010-cls-diagnosis.md`; architecture documented in `blog/CLAUDE.md`. Note: with the SW active only the first post loads on first paint (the rest fall outside the observer's 600px band once kitchen-sink renders to ~3949px) - they load and reveal normally on scroll, this is lazy loading working, not a stall.
