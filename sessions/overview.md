- **Blog OPEN BUG: layout shift. Diagnosed, fix not yet implemented.** Live `/blog/test` measures CLS ~0.298 (threshold 0.1), so Lighthouse Agentic Browsing is 80 in prod while everything else is 100. **Root cause: the stack paints in manifest order but posts render in network-completion order.** All four posts start collapsed, so all fit inside the observer's 600px band and fetch at once; the longest post is first in the stack and slowest to land, and its ~3700px arriving *above* already-painted siblings pushes them out of the viewport. Nothing is collapsing - my earlier reading of the shift rects was wrong, because `LayoutShiftAttribution` rects are clipped to the viewport, so `0x0` means "pushed out of view", not "collapsed". Four fixes failed; height reservation of any kind cannot work (estimates are viewport-width-dependent and wrong by hundreds of px). **Verified fix: a "reveal frontier"** - reveal a post only once it and every post above it have rendered, hiding with `visibility: hidden` so the IntersectionObserver still fires and invisible reflow is excluded from CLS. Measured 0.456 → 0.000 across section load, deep link, heading anchor, and sort toggle under Slow 4G. Full write-up and exact code in `sessions/session-010-cls-diagnosis.md`. **The uncommitted `content-visibility` attempt in the working tree measures 0.456, worse than prod, and should be reverted.**
