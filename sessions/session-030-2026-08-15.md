## Decisions and reasoning

- **Draft, don't delete, the 5 real project stubs and 2 non-project posts**: these are
  real content (in-progress projects, or finished posts just not wanted on the homepage
  right now), not test fixtures - deleting them would lose real work. `draft: true`
  keeps them in the repo and fully recoverable while keeping them off the live site.
- **Delete, don't draft, the 3 fake `agent-hacking` test posts**: these were never real
  content (each body literally says "not real content"), so there's nothing to
  preserve - draft would just leave dead weight in the repo.
- **Tiles are driven by `homepage.json`, not post existence** - this was a discovery
  during the preview, not a design decision made this session, but it's the reason
  "draft the stub" and "hide the tile" are two separate actions, not one.
- **Add `harness-customisation` as a new tile rather than reusing an old slot**: it's a
  genuinely different project (agent/harness tooling, not app development or modding),
  and it already has one real, non-stub post behind it - the bar the user set for what
  deserves a tile at all right now.
